# Skill levels

Four ordered skill levels for intent generation and filtering.

## nontechnical

Users with little or no Git vocabulary. They describe symptoms, panic, or goals in plain language ("my files disappeared", "undo what I just did"). Avoid assuming knowledge of branches, remotes, or flags.

## beginner

Users who know basic Git nouns (commit, branch, push) but not workflows. They ask how-to questions and may misuse common commands. Prefer clear procedural language over jargon.

## intermediate

Users comfortable with daily porcelain (status, add, commit, pull, push, branch, merge). They understand remotes and conflicts at a practical level and can follow multi-step recipes.

## expert

Users fluent in Git mechanics (reflog, rebase, bisect, index vs worktree, object model). They use shorthand, flag names, and precise goals. Prefer concise, high-signal phrasing.
